THE ASSEMBLY

Every economy the World Bank tracks, seated in one hall. Counting delegations…

Why an assembly hall?

Instead of a table of countries, this page turns live World Bank data into a Model UN General Assembly, where every country gets exactly one seat no matter how big it is, just like one country, one vote. Click a seat, call on a delegate by name, or let the chair draw your country like a MUN allocation, and their placard rises with a live briefing built from the World Bank and Wikipedia.
